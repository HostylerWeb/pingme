What we collect

Account details (email or phone, display name, date of birth, gender), profile photo, approximate location while you use PingMe or stay Visible on Wall, messages and wall posts you create, device push tokens, and verification status from our liveness provider.

How we use it

To run proximity features (Wall, Break the ice, matches, chat), keep the community safe (moderation, reports, blocks), send optional notifications, and improve the product.

Location

We use your location to show nearby content and people. Exact coordinates are not shown to other users; they see distance buckets. You can turn off Visible on Wall to stop background presence updates.

Your choices

You can update profile settings, turn off push categories, and export your data from the app. Account deletion is available in Settings and starts a 14-day grace period before permanent removal. During that period you can log back in and cancel deletion.

Account deletion and retention

When you schedule deletion, we revoke active sessions and remove your profile, posts, and login from the service after the grace period. We may retain anonymized safety reports, moderation records, security audit logs, and billing records where required for abuse prevention or legal compliance.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the PingMe operators for your staging environment.
